**ALICIA**

---

**archetype:** duelist

**stats:** str 2 | dex 3 | con 3 | int 2 | wis 3 | cha 3 | save dc 14

**resources:** hp 55 | mp 110 | ac 13 | prof \+3

**weapon:** glass blade (crude magical construct)

identity: versatile omni fairy who adapts between energy/mind/fighter styles mid-combat. picks up whatever tools work and improvises on the fly

---

**passive**

**mystical recovery**

when you successfully save against a spell or magical effect, regain 6 hp OR 6 mp (your choice)

---

**utilities**

**flutter flash**  — utility (2 stars, 10 mp)

commit to a fairy focus for 3 rounds. choose one mode:

* energy mode: \+2 damage to energy attacks, energy attacks apply \-1 incoming modifier

* mind mode: advantage on mind attacks, \+2 ac

* fighter mode: \+3 ac, resistance to physical damage

duration: 3 rounds

**healing hands**  — utility (1 star, 12 mp)

stat: wis

warm pink light flows from your palms. restore 12+wis hp to yourself or an ally within touch range

cooldown: 3 rounds

**psycho parry**  — utility (reaction)

when targeted by a ranged attack, negate it entirely and deal half the incoming damage back at the attacker

uses: 3 per combat

---

**light attacks**

**energy spark**  — light (1 star, 6 mp)

stat: cha | damage: 5

quick burst of crackling energy. on hit, target becomes "energized" for 3 rounds — your energy attacks deal \+2 damage against them. multiple hits refresh the duration

**psycho pull**  — mind / light (1 star, 6 mp)

stat: cha | damage: 4

grip a target with telekinetic force. on hit, they have disadvantage on their next attack

**shimmering strike**  — fighter / light (1 star, 6 mp)

stat: dex | damage: 5

quick thrust with the glass blade. on hit, apply "shimmering" for 1 round — the next attack against them has advantage

---

**medium attacks**

**flower power**  — energy / medium (2 stars, 12 mp)

stat: cha | damage: 4 | 3 hits

multiple flower-shaped energy projectiles burst outward. each hit can target a different enemy. energized targets take \+2 damage per hit

**psycho punchies**  — mind / medium (2 stars, 14 mp)

stat: cha | damage: 10+cha

barrage of invisible telekinetic strikes. on hit, target makes a con save (dc 14\) or is stunned until the start of your next turn

**shattering surge**  — fighter / medium (3 stars, 18 mp)

stat: dex | damage: 11+dex (primary), 6 (fragments)

overcharge the blade and swing. primary target takes full damage. nearby enemies make a dex save (dc 14\) or take fragment damage — half on success

cooldown: 3 rounds

---

**heavy attacks**

**lazor blast**  — energy / heavy (4 stars, 25 mp)

stat: cha | damage: 13+cha

massive beam of concentrated energy. all targets in a line make a dex save (dc 14). on fail: full damage \+ stunned until start of your next turn. on success: half damage. energized targets take \+6 damage

cooldown: 3 rounds

**psychic slam**  — mind / heavy (3 stars, 22 mp)

stat: cha | damage: 10+cha

grab everything nearby with telekinetic force and slam it together. all nearby enemies take damage and make a con save (dc 14\) or are stunned for 1 round. \+4 damage for each additional enemy beyond the first

cooldown: 2 rounds

**giggle fit**  — spell / heavy (3 stars, 18 mp)

stat: cha | damage: 6 per turn

weave a spell of uncontrollable laughter. target makes a wis save (dc 14). on fail: stunned for 2 rounds, takes 6 psychic damage at the start of each turn. on success: disadvantage on their next attack only, no damage

cooldown: 3 rounds

**energy rain**  — energy / heavy (3 stars, 20 mp)

stat: cha | damage: 9 per turn

storm of energy droplets saturates an area. enemies in the area take 9 force damage at the start of their turn. energized targets take \+2 damage. can be dismissed early as a free action

duration: 3 rounds | cooldown: 4 rounds

---

**defensive**

**energy bubble**  — defensive (2 stars, 10 mp)

protective sphere of crackling energy surrounds you. gain 18 temp hp. lasts until depleted or 3 rounds

uses: 3 per combat
