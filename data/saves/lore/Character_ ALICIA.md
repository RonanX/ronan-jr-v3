# **ALICIA**

**archetype:** duelist  
 **stats:** str 1 | dex 4 | con 3 | int 2 | wis 2 | cha 4  
 **resources:** hp 55 | mp 110 | ac 13 | prof \+3  
 **weapon:** glass blade (crude magical construct)

**identity:** versatile omni fairy who adapts between energy/mind/fighter styles mid-combat. picks up whatever tools work and improvises solutions on the fly

---

## **passives**

**mystical recovery**  
 when you successfully save against a spell or magical effect, regain 6 hp OR 6 mp (your choice)

**adaptive arsenal**  
 after using attacks from all 3 fairy styles (energy/mind/fighter) in one combat, your next attack from each style gains advantage and \+4 damage. resets after all 3 bonuses are used

---

## **utility**

**flutter flash (2 stars, 10 mp)**  
 commit to a fairy focus, enhancing your combat style. choose one mode:

* **energy mode:** \+2 damage and “burning” to energy attacks  
* **mind mode:** advantage on mind attacks, \+2 to your ac  
* **fighter mode:** \+3 ac, resistance to physical damage

*duration: 3 rounds*

**healing hands (1 star, 12 mp)**  
 *stat: wis*  
 warm pink light flows from your palms. restore 12+wis hp to yourself or ally within touch range

*cooldown: 3 rounds*

**phase step (1 star, 8 mp)**  
 create afterimages that confuse enemies. gain advantage on your next attack OR use as reaction to impose disadvantage on an attack against you

*uses: 4 per combat*

**energy bubble (2 stars, 10 mp)**  
 protective sphere of crackling energy surrounds you. gain 18 temp hp. can move and act normally while active. lasts until depleted or 3 rounds

*uses: 3 per combat*

---

## **light attacks**

**energy spark \- energy (1 star, 6 mp)**  
 *stat: cha | damage: 5*  
 quick burst of crackling energy. on hit, target becomes "energized" for 3 rounds (your energy attacks deal \+2 damage against them). multiple hits refresh duration

**psycho pull \- mind (1 star, 6 mp)**  
 *stat: cha | damage: 4*  
 grip targets with telekinetic force. hit up to 3 enemies, each one gains disadvantage on their next attack

**shimmering strike \- fighter (1 star, 6 mp)**  
 *stat: dex | damage: 5*  
 quick thrust with the glass blade. on hit, apply "shimmering" effect for 1 round (next attack against them has advantage)

---

## **medium attacks**

**flower power \- energy (2 stars, 12 mp)**  
 *stat: cha | damage: 5 | 3 hits*  
 multiple flower-shaped energy projectiles burst outward. each hit can target different enemies. energized targets take \+2 damage per hit

**psycho punchies \- mind (2 stars, 14 mp)**  
 *stat: cha | damage: 10+cha*  
 barrage of invisible telekinetic strikes. on hit, target makes con save (dc 15\) or stunned until start of your next turn. can use as reaction when targeted by a ranged attack to negate it and strike back

**shattering surge \- fighter (3 stars, 18 mp)**  
 *stat: dex | damage: 11+dex (primary), 6 (fragments)*  
 overcharge the blade and swing. primary target takes full damage. nearby enemies make dex save (dc 15\) or take fragment damage (half on success)

*cooldown: 3 rounds*

---

## **heavy attacks**

**lazor blast \- energy (4 stars, 25 mp)**  
 *stat: cha | damage: 16+cha*  
 massive beam of concentrated energy. all targets in line make dex save (dc 15). on fail: full damage \+ stunned until start of your next turn. on success: half damage. energized targets take \+6 damage. destroys cover/barriers

*cooldown: 3 rounds*

**psychic slam \- mind (3 stars, 22 mp)**  
 *stat: cha | damage: 10+cha*  
 grab everything nearby with telekinetic force and slam it together. all nearby enemies take damage and make con save (dc 15\) or stunned for 1 round. \+4 damage for each additional enemy beyond the first

*cooldown: 2 rounds*

**giggle fit \- spell (3 stars, 18 mp)**  
 *stat: cha | damage: 6 per turn*  
 weave a curse that induces uncontrollable laughter. target makes wis save (dc 15). on fail: stunned for 2 rounds, takes 6 psychic damage at start of each turn. on success: disadvantage on next attack only, no damage

*cooldown: 3 rounds*

**energy rain \- energy (3 stars, 20 mp)**  
 *stat: cha | damage: 9 per turn*  
 storm of falling energy droplets saturates an area. enemies in area take 9 force damage at start of their turn. energized targets take \+2 damage. can dismiss early as free action

*duration: 3 rounds | cooldown: 4 rounds*
