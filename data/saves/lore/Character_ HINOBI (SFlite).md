# Base Form

---

# **HIN'OBI \- BASE FORM: CROWD CONTROL**

**archetype:** duelist  
 **stats:** str 2 | dex 3 | con 2 | int 3 | wis 2 | cha 3  
 **resources:** hp 55 | mp 110 | ac 14 | prof \+3  
 **weapon:** saber

**form swap cost:** 1 star

**identity:** mobile crowd controller who pressures groups through multi-target attacks and defensive layers. weaker in pure 1v1 but has tools to maintain pressure

---

## **passive**

**one-man army**  
 when fighting multiple enemies (gm determines), gain \+2 ac

---

## **moves**

**cloud formation \- utility (2 stars, 10 mp)**  
 summon storm afterimages across the battlefield. gain advantage on attacks as you strike from multiple angles  
 duration: 2 rounds

**static shield \- utility (2 stars, 10 mp)**  
 reaction when targeted. gain \+3 ac vs that attack. if they still hit, they take 6 lightning damage. you can still use light attacks this turn

**tempest fang \- light (1 star)**  
 *stat: dex | damage: 4 | 2 hits*  
 double backfist \- body then face. each successful hit gives attackers disadvantage vs you until your next turn (stacks)

**storm drummer \- medium (2 stars, 10 mp)**  
 *stat: dex | damage: 3 | 4 hits*  
 electrified chain punches. on clean hit, target is stunned until start of your next turn

**thunderlash \- medium (2 stars, 12 mp)**  
 *stat: int | damage: 8+int lightning*  
 drive saber into ground, electricity erupts in the area. hit targets are marked \- attacking . they take 3 damage from your attacks for 2 rounds  
 *cooldown: 3 rounds*

**licér \- medium (2 stars, 12 mp)**  
 *stat: int | damage: 12*  
 concentrated lightning bolt strikes single target

**licér, wi rós \- heavy (4 stars, 18 mp)**  
 *stat: int | damage: 5 per round on failed save*  
 raging typhoon engulfs area. all targets make dex save each round or take damage. while active, targets have disadvantage on attacks due to difficult terrain  
 *duration: 3 rounds |  cooldown: 3 rounds*

**hyperactive reflexes**

---

**twin cyclones \- utility (2 stars, 10 mp)**  
 gap closer that creates an afterimage. target makes dc 10 dex save. on fail, your next attack has advantage and deals \+4 damage

**wall cloud \- utility (2 stars, 10 mp)**  
 swords blur into a defensive wall. immune to most small ranged attacks  
 *duration: 2 rounds*

**calm before \- utility (2 stars, 10 mp)**  
 reaction when targeted. auto-dodge attack, leave afterimage that deals 4 damage to attacker. can immediately reposition (narrative)

**approaching storm \- light (1 star, 3 mp)**  
 *stat: dex | damage: 5*  
 momentum-fueled strike. if this is your first attack this turn, gain advantage on the roll

**gentle breeze \- light (1 star, 4 mp)**  
 *stat: dex | damage: 4 (auto-hit)*

 *cooldown: 1 round*

**eye of the storm \- medium (2 stars, 12 mp)**

 bypasses shields and frontal defense. if target attempted to block, they lose their next reaction

**gust front \- medium (2 stars, 12 mp)**  
 *stat: dex | damage: 6*  
 on hit, your next attack this turn deals \+6 damage and ignores one defensive ability

**perfect storm \- heavy (4 stars, 25 mp)**  
 stat: dex | damage:  | 4 hits  
 make 4 light attacks in rapid succession (roll separately). each successful hit after the first deals \+2 damage  
 *cooldown: 4 rounds*

# God Breaker

---

# **HIN'OBI \- GOD BREAKER: APEX PREDATOR**

**archetype:** duelist (berserker variant)  
 **stats:** str 4 | dex 5 | con 2 | int 3 | wis 1 | cha 3  
 **resources:** hp 55 | mp 100 | ac 16 | prof \+4  
 **weapon:** plasma halberd (long reach, ignores barriers)

**form swap cost:** 1 star, 10 mp, 5 prior swaps  
 **transformation:** restore to full hp and mp on activation  
 **duration:** until mp depletes (loses 5 per round)  
 **lockout:** cannot transform for 5 rounds after reverting

**identity:** adaptive berserker who learns from every hit taken and grows stronger as battle continues. combines all previous forms' techniques with plasma-wreathed precision

---

## **passive**

**apex evolution**  
 whenever you miss an attack, fail a save, or take 5+ damage in one hit:

* gain \+1 to the relevant stat for rest of combat  
* gain 1 evolution stack (max 5\)

additionally:

* below 50% hp: \+2 to all stats  
* below 25% hp: \+4 to all stats (stacks with evolution bonuses)
